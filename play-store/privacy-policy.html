Privacy Policy for Vehicle Maintenance Tracker App

Effective October 2, 2026

This app keeps everything you enter on your device. This policy explains what it stores and where that information can go.

What the app stores

The vehicles, maintenance items, service and repair records, costs, mileage, and notes you enter. They are kept only in the app's private storage on your device.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It does not request internet access, so it cannot send your information anywhere, and the developer never receives any of it.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. Google runs that backup, not the developer, and you can turn it off in your device's settings.

Files you export or share

When you export a backup, or save, print, or share a service history PDF, the file goes only to the place or app you choose.

Reminders

Service reminders are notifications created on your device. You can turn them off in your device's settings.

Deleting your information

Deleting a vehicle removes it and everything logged for it. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device. Files you exported and any Android backup are separate, and stay until you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted in the app and at the same web address, with a new effective date.
